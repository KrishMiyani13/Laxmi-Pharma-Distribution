About Laxmi Pharma Distributor

Bridging Pharmaceutical Science & Gujarat's Healthcare Supply Chain

What We Do

"We buy pharmaceutical and medical products from manufacturers and supply them to pharmacies, hospitals, clinics, stockists and sub-distributors, with the aim of making medicine supply simple, dependable and fairly priced."

Company Overview

Personal Attention of a Local Partner. Discipline of a Modern Supply Chain.

Laxmi Pharma Distributor is a growing pharmaceutical distribution company based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. We connect trusted pharmaceutical manufacturers with the healthcare providers who depend on them: pharmacies, hospitals, clinics, stockists and distributors.

We source medicines and medical products directly from authorized manufacturers and deliver them with a focus on authenticity, timely supply and dependable service. We currently represent 6 manufacturers and handle over 500 products across injectables, solid dosage forms and other formulations.

As a growing firm, our customers deal directly with people who know their needs.

Our Story

Laxmi Pharma Distributor was started with a simple idea: healthcare providers should be able to get genuine medicines, on time, from a distributor they can trust. Beginning with direct manufacturer authorizations and a close-knit customer base, we have steadily expanded our product range and the network of pharmacies, hospitals and distributors we serve in Ahmedabad and across Gujarat.
