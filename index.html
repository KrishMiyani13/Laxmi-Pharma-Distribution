About Laxmi Distribution Pharma Plus

Company Overview

Laxmi Distribution Pharma Plus is a growing pharmaceutical distribution company based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. We source medicines and medical products directly from authorized manufacturers and deliver them with a focus on authenticity, timely supply and dependable service.

We currently represent 6 authorized manufacturers and handle over 500 products across injectables, solid dosage forms and specialty formulations. As a growing firm, we combine the personal attention of a local business with the discipline of a professional supply chain. Our customers deal directly with people who know their needs.

Our Story

Laxmi Distribution Pharma Plus was started with a simple idea: healthcare providers should be able to get genuine medicines, on time, from a distributor they can trust. Beginning with direct authorized manufacturer relationships and a loyal local customer base, we have steadily expanded our product range and the network of pharmacies, hospitals and distributors we serve in Ahmedabad and across Gujarat.

Laxmi Distribution Pharma Plus is dedicated to the safe, honest and reliable supply of medicines. We believe every pharmacy, hospital and clinic should be able to depend on genuine products delivered on time, so that patients receive the care they need.

Validated 2°C – 8°C Cold-Chain Infrastructure

Temperature excursions destroy the therapeutic efficacy of biologicals, insulins, vaccines, and chemotherapy injectables. At Laxmi Pharma Distribution, cold-chain is not an afterthought—it is a closed-loop engineering standard.

Pre-Conditioned Phase-Change Shippers

High-density expanded polyurethane (EPU) cooler boxes with certified gel packs that maintain 2°C–8°C for up to 36 hours in extreme Gujarat summers.

Calibrated Single-Use Digital Data Loggers

Each institutional shipment includes a USB/Bluetooth temperature logger. Receiving hospital pharmacists can audit temperature curves before signing delivery challans.

Dedicated Temperature-Controlled Transit

Insulated delivery vehicles equipped with backup power generators to eliminate temperature spikes during interstate highway transits.
